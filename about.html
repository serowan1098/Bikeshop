Uncompromising Quality & Value

Founded under the vision of Sir Christian Birot, Sero'1s SS (Shop) was established to bridge the gap between elite international cycling engineering and passionate riders across the Philippines.

We refuse to compromise on build quality, safety, or rider fit. Every road bike, track machine, racing kit, and component in our catalog is rigorously evaluated for performance and longevity.

"You Get What You Pay For."

This principle guides everything we do. We believe in educating cyclists on carbon fiber grades, aerodynamic gains, drivetrain efficiency, and proper sizing so every investment delivers true value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our catalog, inquiries, and showroom standards.

Is this an online checkout store?

No. Sero'1s SS (Shop) is an exclusive product catalog and inquiry platform. We encourage personalized consultations and direct inquiries to ensure proper sizing and component compatibility.

How do product inquiries work?

Browse our road, track, apparel, or component catalogs, click "View & Inquire" on any product, and fill out our inquiry form. Our team will contact you to discuss availability and showroom viewing.

Are specifications and prices accurate?

All prices are listed in Philippine Pesos (₱) and reflect current demonstration inventory. Specifications are sourced directly from premier manufacturer guidelines.
